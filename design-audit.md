# Gemora Landing Page Design Audit (Feb 2026)

## Visual Identity
- **Primary Color:** Deep Purple / Indigo (Gemora brand color).
- **CTA Color:** Purple background with white text, often accompanied by an arrow icon (⚡️ or 🦾).
- **Typography:** Modern, clean sans-serif. High contrast headings.
- **Components:**
    - **Header:** Transparent/Blurred backdrop, logo on left, nav in center, primary CTAs ("Log ind", "Kom i gang") on right.
    - **Cards:** Rounded corners, subtle shadows, often featuring a small icon/badge (e.g., "Chat", "Feedback").
    - **Typography Hero:** Big, bold headings with specific words highlighted (e.g., "superkræfter.").
    - **FAQ:** Accordion style (simple +/- toggles).

## Content Tone
- Professional but approachable.
- Focus on "lærere" (teachers), "elever" (students), and "indsigt" (insight).
- Value-driven: "Løft kvaliteten", "Spar tid", "Dybere feedback".

## Landing Page Structure Strategy for SEO
For each subject-specific landing page (e.g., /fag/dansk), we will replicate this structure:
1. **Hero:** Big bold title (Gemora for [Subject]) + Value proposition.
2. **Feature Grid:** 3 cards showing specific use-cases for that subject (e.g., Grammar, Analysis, Exams).
3. **Product Demo Blocks:** Alternating text/image blocks mimicking the main page's "Gemora for Chat/Feedback" sections.
4. **FAQ:** Subject-specific questions.
5. **CTA:** Large footer section "Prøv Gemora gratis".
