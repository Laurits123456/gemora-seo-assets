# SEO Content Loop Strategy: The Subject Deep-Dive (2026 Edition)

Goal: Dominate organic search for AI in the Danish educational sector by creating specific, high-quality landing pages for every subject/topic combination.

## Phase 1: Topic Mapping
- **Dansk:** Litterær analyse, Saga-læsning, Dokumentarisme, Argumentationsanalyse, Skriftlig fremstilling.
- **Matematik:** Differentialregning, Statistik, Sandsynlighedsregning, Geometri, Integralregning.
- **Engelsk/Sprog:** Grammatik-træning, Essay writing, Cultural analysis, Text interpretation.
- **Samfundsfag:** Økonomisk politik, International politik, Sociologi, Metodelære.
- **Historie:** Kildekritik, Periodisering, Historiebrug, Global historie.

## Content Structure per Page
1. **Introduction:** Why AI is relevant for this specific topic.
2. **Pedagogical Value:** How Gemora helps teachers provide better feedback here.
3. **Practical Examples:** Real-world prompts or feedback scenarios.
4. **Compliance & Ethics:** Why Gemora is safer than raw LLMs for this subject.
5. **Call to Action:** Try Gemora Ultra for your next lesson.

## Execution
- Content will be generated in markdown.
- Metadata will be updated in `seo_metadata.json`.
- Sitemap will be expanded automatically.
